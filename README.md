## About me

I am a **Software & AI Engineer**, working at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, education and industrial innovation.

- Master's student in **Information Engineering at UFABC**, researching **Explainable AI (XAI)** and **Computer Vision** applied to the assessment of practical technical skills.
- More than 12 years of experience across full-stack development, software architecture, AI, data platforms, UX/UI and digital products.
- Former **WorldSkills Expert — Skill 08: Mobile Applications Development**.
- **Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Engineer Associate**.
- Experience designing software and data architectures for applied R&D, industrial digitalization, cloud platforms and AI-enabled systems.
- I also teach and help technical teams adopt engineering practices and AI-assisted development with quality, security and governance.
